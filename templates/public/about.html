What is Inktrovert?

One place for the tattoo and piercing world — the people who collect ink and the professionals who put it there.

The magazine

Inktrovert is a digital magazine covering the tattoo and piercing industry: artist spotlights, shop stories, convention news, new products, and the work collectors are actually getting done. Issues land on a regular rhythm, and every back issue stays readable forever.

The directory

Find tattoo artists, piercers, studios, and trusted brands. Artists and shops can claim their listings, show their portfolios and flash, and keep their details current — so the listing you find is the shop that actually exists. Verified badges mark the ones who've confirmed they're the real deal.

The pro network

Industry pros get a professional home here: podcasts on craft and business, continuing education content, a supply marketplace for pro-to-pro gear, and — coming soon — tools that make booking through the platform possible.

Why it exists

Collectors want to find great artists. Artists want to be found by the right clients. Shops want to be seen for what they really are. Inktrovert puts all of that in one place instead of scattered across a dozen apps that don't understand the industry.
